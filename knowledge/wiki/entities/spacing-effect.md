## TL;DR
The spacing effect is the phenomenon where learning is more effective when study sessions are spaced out over time, rather than crammed together. It's the core principle behind spaced repetition.

## Body
Research shows that spreading out study or practice sessions leads to better long-term retention than massed practice. The spacing effect has been demonstrated across many domains, from vocabulary to procedural skills, and is a key reason why spaced repetition works so well.
