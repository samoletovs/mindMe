## TL;DR
Hermann Ebbinghaus was a German scientist who first conceptualized spaced repetition and developed the 'forgetting curve,' laying the foundation for modern memory research.

## Body
In the 1880s, Ebbinghaus conducted pioneering experiments on memory, showing how information is forgotten over time and how spaced reviews can slow this process. His work is the basis for much of what we know about effective learning and memory retention today.
