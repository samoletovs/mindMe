## TL;DR
The Leitner system is a manual flashcard method for spaced repetition, where cards are sorted into groups based on how well I know them. Less familiar cards are reviewed more often, while well-known cards are reviewed less frequently.

## Body
Devised by Sebastian Leitner, the Leitner system uses a series of boxes or partitions. When I answer a card correctly, it moves to the next box (reviewed less often); if I get it wrong, it goes back to the first box (reviewed most often). This simple system helps focus my study time on the material I struggle with most, and naturally spaces out reviews for things I've already learned.
