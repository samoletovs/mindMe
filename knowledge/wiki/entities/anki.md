## TL;DR
Anki is a popular, customizable flashcard app that uses spaced repetition algorithms to help me remember information long-term. It's open-source and widely used for language learning, exams, and technical subjects.

## Body
Anki lets me create my own flashcards and schedules reviews based on how well I recall each card. It supports plugins and different algorithms (like FSRS) for fine-tuning the learning process. Anki is flexible and works across devices, making it easy to integrate into daily routines.
