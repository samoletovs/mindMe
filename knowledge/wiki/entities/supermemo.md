## TL;DR
SuperMemo is a software program that implements advanced spaced repetition algorithms to optimize review schedules. It's one of the earliest and most influential digital tools for this learning technique.

## Body
SuperMemo was developed by Piotr Wozniak and introduced algorithmic scheduling for spaced repetition, adjusting review intervals based on my performance. The SM family of algorithms has influenced many other tools, including Anki. SuperMemo is especially popular among language learners and anyone who needs to memorize large amounts of information efficiently.
