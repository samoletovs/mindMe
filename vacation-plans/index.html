⭐ My recommendation: China

China wins on the two things that matter most for your family: everyone can enter visa-free (your wife's Russian passport is the dealbreaker for the US), and it is roughly €2,000–3,000 cheaper for the same two weeks. It also delivers huge kid appeal — the Great Wall, the Terracotta Warriors and the panda base. New York is wonderful, but only attempt it if you start your wife's US visa application immediately and it is confirmed by late September; otherwise the trip could collapse at the last minute.
