Why China for your family

The deciding factor — everyone gets in with no visa. Latvian citizens (you + the kids) have 30-day visa-free entry to China, valid through 31 Dec 2026. Your wife's Russian passport also gets 30-day visa-free entry under the Russia–China bilateral arrangement. No fees, no interviews, no waiting — a stark contrast to the US, where her passport would require a B1/B2 visa with months of uncertainty.
  • Cost-effective: the flights are the only big expense; food, trains and hotels are cheap.
  • Kid-friendly wow: Great Wall, Terracotta Warriors and a giant-panda base in one trip.
  • Easy internal travel: fast, comfortable bullet trains connect every city.

What to take into consideration

🛂 Documents

  • All four passports valid 6+ months beyond travel, with a blank page.
  • Re-confirm visa-free terms before booking (embassy of China in Latvia; wife's Russia–China terms).
  • Carry printed hotel + return-flight confirmations.

💳 Money & payments

  • China is largely cashless — set up Alipay and/or WeChat Pay and link a card before you fly.
  • Carry a little cash as backup; notify your bank of travel.

📱 Connectivity

  • Install a reputable VPN before arrival if you want your usual apps.
  • Buy a travel eSIM or roaming; download offline maps + a translation app (Pleco / Google Translate offline).

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family practicalities

  • Book Great Wall, Terracotta Warriors, Panda Base and popular trains a few days ahead.
  • Passports double as ID at train stations — keep them handy.
  • Pack layers + comfy walking shoes; bring any regular medicines.
  • Clear the ~1 week of school absence with teachers in advance.

Booking order (to lock the best prices)

  1. Flights first — Riga↔China for 17 Oct / 31 Oct; set fare alerts and book once a good fare appears.
  2. Hotels — refundable rooms in all four cities (free-cancellation gives flexibility).
  3. Internal transport — reserve key bullet trains / the Chengdu→Shanghai flight ~1–2 weeks out.
  4. Attractions — pre-book the Great Wall, Warriors and Panda Base.
  5. Insurance + eSIM + Alipay setup — the week before departure.
