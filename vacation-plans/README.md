# Family Vacation 2026 — China vs. New York

Comprehensive, research-based plans for a **2-week family trip** (2 adults + kids aged 12 & 9),
based in **Riga**, travelling **Sat 17 Oct → Sun 1 Nov 2026** (Latvian autumn break + one excused week).

## Open the plans

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any browser for the overview, then drill into:

- [`china.html`](china.html) — 🇨🇳 **Recommended.** Beijing · Xi'an · Chengdu · Shanghai. Visa-free for the whole
  family. Estimated **€6,000–7,200**.
- [`new-york.html`](new-york.html) — 🗽 NYC + Washington DC. Only viable if the wife's US visa (Russian citizen)
  is confirmed in time. Estimated **€8,200–10,400**.

## Bottom line

**China is the recommended choice:** everyone enters visa-free (the wife's Russian passport rules out an easy US
trip), it is ~€2,000–3,000 cheaper, and it offers huge kid appeal (Great Wall, Terracotta Warriors, pandas).
Pursue New York only if the wife's B1/B2 visa is started immediately and confirmed by late September 2026.
